<!--suicide attempt--><BR>
<BR>
I was feeling miserable one day so I took a whole load of tablets.<BR>
Within two hours I started feeling a little spaced out but was still able to wander around.<BR>
I rode my bicycle up to the post office to get some money and then went around to the Clubhouse, where my friends noticed that I was a little spacey.<BR>
I told them what I had done and they drove me up to the Accident & Emergency department. I apparently had three seizures - fortunately, I don't remember them.<BR>
I was then transferred to a medical ward, for observation and assessment.<BR>
By now, I was feeling quite happy with myself and was able to chat with other people on the ward.<BR>
Unfortunately, four days later I seriously lost it<BR>
I was convinced that the staff were playing out a game, where half of them worked for the Devil, putting temptations in people's way, hoping to get them to the 'Dark Side' and the other half working for God. Some of them were also double agents, pretending to work for each other.<BR>
I thought that I had done something bad and that I was going to be taken to the hospital basement (where Hell was) and I screamed for the help of passers-by to stop them.<BR>
After grabbing at staff members to take me home with them, Security was called and I was given two injections. This eventually calmed me down and I was driven to the local psychiatric hospital, where I stayed for just short of a week.<BR>
I am now receiving an injection of some type of anti-psychotic medicine every three weeks  probably for the rest of my life.<BR>
I would seriously advise anyone trying to commit suicide (or even just thrill-seekers) not to attempt an overdose with this drug. That Monday was the scariest day of my life and not one I will soon forget!<BR>
I understand that the Solanacea family of plants (Belladonna, Datura etc) create a similar effect of detachment from reality and it should be pointed out that I have experimented with Brugmansia and travel sickness tablets, recently. This may have contributed to my episode so I will not be doing that again, either.
