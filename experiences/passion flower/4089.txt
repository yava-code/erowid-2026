About a year ago a friend and I acquired some of the ground substance in question. I am not sure the total amount taken but it was around 2 large capsules worth each at the beginning. We dropped around 7pm and drank some beers. Nothing special happened but a light euphoric feeling and heightened awareness, so we dropped 2 more each. We decided to play some poker with a group of friends and then about 20 minutes later went outside for a smoke. It was outside that it hit me. First it started out slow but we could definitely feel it creeping up on us. Someone said something to me but I was not paying any attention because I was too busy staring at the glowing blue light billowing off the house next door. Then he repeated the question, but then it was like a repeat of what had already happened without the thought of me knowing I had trailed off on the blue-light. Then I said something that didn't make any sense. <BR>
<BR>
So we went back inside and continued playing poker. The game ensued and due to a losing hand, arguing became loud and as sharp and piercing as blades slicing my ears into my brain. It seemed like I was with a pack of mad dogs and had to get out of there. I was too complacent to sit there and let them slice my head open. Then they looked at me and everything went silent. But then it was like it slowed down and started back up again. Like time was freezing in intervals and I was beyond the time during the freezing period of absolute silence. The arguing was even more intense as it seemed as they were saying more at once than was actually said. Like I could not only hear what they were saying, but also hear what they werent saying, what they were going to say, and what they hadnt said yet. Then it got worse and too chaotic and my tripping friend and I went upstairs to listen to someone play acoustic guitar. But as I walked up the stairs, it was like I was at the top of the stairs before I even had experienced being at the bottom, but then I was at the bottom, then in the middle, then back at the bottom, then at the top again. Everything seemed larger than normal and at times going normal speed, slowing down and then faster than normal speed. The tiny cracker-box room we went into seemed to grow as I paced myself around the room listening to some soothing acoustic guitar that made my mind at peace again. <BR>
<BR>
Things seemed normal again, but it also seemed like I had shrunk half my size, and everything was abnormally large. Then we went back downstairs to find everyone gone, and a silent room. We sat on the most comfortable couch. The more I sat on it, the more comfy it became. It seemed like I was becoming one with the couch as I was so comfortable that I could not move. I kept sinking lower and deeper into the couch, each time feeling more content with the couch. It was like I was paralyzed and could not move. Suddenly, I heard music again, but it was old diner jazz music or something coming from somewhere. I looked and saw an old radio, the style from the 20's. Somehow I felt like I was in a diner from the 20's, with this music going, looking out of a window. It was like I was in my body, so comfortable I went into this ultra-reality where I saw myself lying on the couch but then I was looking out the window of a diner drinking a cup of coffee. The music made no sense to me, but just made me relax until I lost consciousness and awoke around 3am feeling mostly normal except the feeling of being hyper and unable to sleep. <BR>
<BR>
Overall, no long-term effects showed up the following day, except for a more determined-to-get-shit-done outlook on life. It was very spiritual in a sense. I have taken tons of shitty and good acid in my day, and had good and bad trips. I have also had plenty of good experiences with 'Shrooms and many a bad experience with 30+ Merazine or Dramamine, but this was unlike anything I have ever had before. I have never had such a feeling of euphoria before. I was as though I was in a dreamland of the waking, yet not really knowing for sure if I was awake.
