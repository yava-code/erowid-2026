In my early developmental years I played with many ideas for an easy to create bong that worked well.  The materials I used:<br/>
<br/>
1. One pill container (prescription drugstore kind)<br/>
2. Two caps that fit the bottle<br/>
3. 1/4 in tubing, hollow pen works<br/>
4. One ring that fits around the tubing (available at Home Depot)<br/>
5. Aluminum foil<br/>
<br/>
Directions:<br/>
First I take a pair of scissors and cut the bottle from the bottom to my desired height of my base.  Once completed, I take a lighter and burn the plastic right above the screw indentations, and take an old pen and make a hole.  I take ake the ring and stick it through the hole, the tighter the better.  Now, I take A LOT of aluminum foil and wrap the inside, make sure all the plastic  is completely covered.  Once done, I take the second cap (the first cap is on the bottle) and burn a half-circle into it so that it can fit around the ring that I previously placed in the base.  I take the tubing and insert it into the ring, forming a pipe, bong, etc.  <br/>
<br/>
Great hits can be taken off of this, I was just messing around with some materials one day and found that this easy bong works well.  If I can't get a pipe, no problem.  <!--Just simply follow these directions and you'll be set. --><br/>
<br/>
Once I smoke up, I cover the top of the bowl with the second cap so that the smoke is trapped and has nowhere to go put through the tube when I suck on it, which allows for bigger and better hits.
